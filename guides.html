How a walk goes

1 A request comes in

Someone picks your area and a length. You see what they want before you accept — and declining costs you nothing.

2 You walk, they steer

Directions land on your screen as big prompts with a buzz. Turn left. Get closer. What’s that. You talk, they listen.

3 Nothing is recorded

No video is stored, ever. Not by us, not by them. It matters for the people you film without asking — vendors, passers-by.

4 You end it whenever

Uncomfortable, unsafe, or just done — end the walk and keep every minute you earned. No explanation required.
